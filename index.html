WARM-UP The Master of Excuses
8 min
1
You forgot my birthday!
Your friend is devastated. You forgot their birthday completely. Give a creative excuse!
💡 Follow-up: "But why didn't you call me at least?"
2
You were 2 hours late!
You arrived 2 hours late to an important dinner. Your host is furious. Explain yourself!
💡 Follow-up: "Two hours?! What could possibly take that long?"
3
You didn't do the homework!
The teacher is waiting for your homework. You didn't do it. Be creative — no boring excuses!
💡 Follow-up: "That's the third time this month! Really?"
4
You ate the last slice!
You ate the last slice of cake that was saved for someone else. They are very upset!
💡 Follow-up: "I specifically told you that was mine!"
5
You missed the flight!
The whole group missed the flight because of you. Everyone is at the airport, furious!
💡 Follow-up: "We had to buy new tickets because of you!"
6
You lost the hotel key!
You lost the hotel room key and now everyone is locked out at midnight. Explain!
💡 Follow-up: "It's midnight! How did this happen?"
7
You broke my sunglasses!
You borrowed your friend's expensive sunglasses on vacation and came back with them broken. They are not happy!
💡 Follow-up: "Those were $200! What exactly happened?"
8
You spent all the cash!
You were in charge of the group's vacation money. You spent it all in one day. Everyone is broke!
💡 Follow-up: "We still have 4 days left! What were you thinking?"
9
You gave wrong directions!
You were the navigator. Because of your directions, the group got completely lost for 3 hours. Explain!
💡 Follow-up: "We missed the tour we paid for! How do you explain that?"
10
You booked the wrong hotel!
You were in charge of reservations. You booked a hotel in the wrong city. The group just found out!
💡 Follow-up: "The hotel is 200 km away! How is that even possible?"
11
You forgot the passports!
You were responsible for carrying the group's passports. You left them at home. You're at the airport!
💡 Follow-up: "The flight boards in 40 minutes! What do we do now?"
12
You posted that photo!
You posted an embarrassing vacation photo of your friend online without asking. They just saw it!
💡 Follow-up: "300 people liked it already! Take it down NOW!"
💡 Example Answer
Scenario: "You were 2 hours late!"
"OK, I know this sounds crazy — on my way here I found a dog stuck in a tree. I waited for animal control, but the dog jumped down on its own and ran away! I chased it to make sure it was safe, then got a parking ticket arguing with the officer. I'm so sorry, but what would YOU have done?"
SLANG PRESENTATION Slang Spotlight
Book p. None · 8 min
1
It was a total blast!
It was extremely fun and exciting
"The trip to Thailand? It was a total blast!"
2
It was so worth it!
The effort or cost was completely justified
"The 12-hour flight? It was so worth it!"
3
Hit the road!
To start a trip or leave a place
"We packed our bags and hit the road at 5 AM!"
4
Go with the flow!
To be flexible and adapt to whatever happens
"No plan? No problem — we just went with the flow!"
5
It's a no-brainer!
An obvious decision that requires no thinking
"Go to Cancún or stay home? It's a no-brainer!"

💡 Example Argument
Destination: Paris vs Tokyo
"OK, so I was in Paris last summer and it was absolutely incredible. The food was amazing — we went to this tiny café near the Eiffel Tower and honestly, it was so worth it. We didn't have a plan at all — we just went with the flow and discovered the most beautiful streets. The atmosphere was romantic, the museums were fascinating, and the people were surprisingly friendly. Paris was not just a city — it was an experience. Tokyo is great, but Paris? It was a total blast from start to finish!"
